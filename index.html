App market signals digest

For each iOS app in a list: its best App Store chart rank and the move since yesterday, live and newly launched paid ads with 8 weeks of ad starts, organic post views, and how many searches it ranks top 10 for. Paste one market signals response below. Nothing leaves your browser.

Where to get the JSON

The input is the market signals response from PeekPanda, an iOS competitor catalogue that keeps dated chart, paid-ad, organic and keyword evidence for each app. Send up to 600 numeric App Store ids per request:

curl --get https://api.peekpanda.com/v1/market/signals \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PEEKPANDA_TOKEN" \
  -H 'x-genviral-tenancy: personal' \
  --data-urlencode 'country=us' \
  --data-urlencode 'storeIds=1000000001,1000000002'

Over MCP this is the get_market tool with action signals. You need a PeekPanda account and an API key; the market signals reference lists the fields, limits and errors. You can also paste a JSON array of several responses (one per batch).

How to read it

  • Best chart rank is the best rank across every stored App Store chart of the storefront on the latest chart day. ▲/▼ is the move since the day before.
  • "no ads found" means a recent collection found none. "not collected" means no collection has finished, so it says nothing about the app's ads.
  • The bars show paid-ad starts for 8 Monday-start weeks, oldest first. The last week is still running.
  • Organic posts are matched by product name, not account ownership. Search positions are observed results, not search volume.
  • "unknown" means that stream was never collected for the app. It is unknown, not zero.
